I've completed a full final verification pass on the BESU Customs AI Designer and attached a

The designer now follows the full concept workflow: customers submit their brief, receive four
distinct uniform concepts, select the direction they prefer, and then refine only that selected

While verifying I also found and fixed two smaller issues: the production PNG and PDF exports were

I rechecked the responsive experience on desktop, laptop, tablet and phone, along with the
generation and refinement flow, colour variations, the roster, production exports and the Shopify
handoff. Everything in the report was verified by driving the actual application in a browser
rather than by reading the code.

Importantly, I ran the whole journey twice: once through our internal preview renderer, so the
regression suite can be re-run repeatedly without burning image credits, and once end to end against
the live AI. Both runs passed all 32 browser checks. The report shows the real live AI uniforms so
you can see exactly what a customer receives — one GALACTIC brief produced four genuinely different
finished designs, each with the jersey and shorts front and back, the team name on the chest and a
clean number area on the back.

Running against live AI also surfaced two things the preview renderer could never show, because they
are behaviours of the image model itself. Both are in the report in detail:

- On some renders the model was adding a sportswear manufacturer's logo to a blank part of the
  jersey or shorts. That is obviously not acceptable on a customer's uniform, so I tightened the
  generation rules to prohibit any brand mark outright. Fresh renders came back clean and I checked
  them zoomed in.

- The team name on the chest occasionally lost a letter. I tightened that too, and generation now
  spells it correctly every time. One case remains: when a customer recolours a design, the model
  redraws the lettering and can still swap a single character. The clean fix is to stop asking the AI
  to draw the team name at all and instead print it ourselves from the team-name field, so it is
  always exactly right. It is a contained change and I would recommend we do it — happy to get it
  done on your go-ahead.
